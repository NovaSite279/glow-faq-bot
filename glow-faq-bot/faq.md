## Hours
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 to 18:00
- Saturday: 8:00 to 15:00
- Closed on Sundays and public holidays

## Location
- Shop 12, Willow Square, Sandton
- Free basement parking

## Booking
- Book by WhatsApp message or by calling 011 000 0000
- Walk-ins are welcome when there is space
- Saturdays are usually fully booked

## Prices
All prices are "from" prices. The final price is confirmed at the consultation.
- Women's cut and blow-dry: from R350
- Men's cut: R180
- Kids' cut (under 12): R120
- Colour: from R650
- Highlights: from R950
- Braids: from R600
- Treatments: from R250

## How long it takes
- Cut: about 45 minutes
- Colour: 2 to 3 hours
- Braids: 3 to 6 hours

## Deposit
- R100 deposit for colour and braid bookings
- Refundable if cancelled at least 24 hours before

## Cancellations
- 24 hours' notice is needed, otherwise the deposit is forfeited

## Payment
- Card, EFT or cash

## Bridal and events
- Packages on request
- A consultation is required
- Book at least 4 weeks ahead

## Gift vouchers
- Available in store or via WhatsApp
- Valid for 6 months

## Products
